Privacy information

Nazar Begen, the creator of xSonomy, uses your email address and message to review your suggestion and reply where needed. Your submission is stored privately in Supabase and a copy is emailed to brntaut@gmail.com through Resend. Feedback is not published automatically and your email is not added to a marketing list.

We record the wording and time of your consent. Spam protection uses a short-lived browser challenge and a hashed connection identifier; raw IP addresses are not stored in the feedback table. The hashed identifier is cleared after the rate-limit window. We keep the message while it is needed for review and follow-up. To ask about your data or request deletion, email brntaut@gmail.com.

Please avoid sending passwords, confidential documents or other sensitive information. You can withdraw your consent by contacting the same address.

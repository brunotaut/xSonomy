About these insights

Company charts use the reconciled, published landscape and each company's headquarters country. They count organisations, not products, market revenue or manufacturing sites. Country totals and the three domain groups count each company once.

Capability assignments may overlap: a company can appear under several technologies. The heatmap uses the eight largest HQ countries and six most represented capabilities within the selected geography.

The network chart counts independent hub organisations with a published location and investors with a published office/contact city. Associated hub sites, meeting-only locations and country research associations are excluded. A multi-country organisation can appear in several country rows. Directory coverage is incomplete.

The events chart covers the current month and following eleven months, counting each confirmed dated edition once by its start month. Ongoing editions are counted in the current month. Past editions and dates TBA are excluded. Future editions remain available in Events; zero means no confirmed edition in our current database.

Figures refresh with each catalogue build. These charts describe the available records; the dataset continues to expand.

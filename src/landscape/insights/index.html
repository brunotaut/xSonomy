About these insights

Company charts use the reconciled, published landscape and each company's headquarters country. They count organisations, not products, market revenue or manufacturing sites. Country totals and the three domain groups count each company once.

Capability assignments may overlap: a company can appear under several technologies. The heatmap uses the eight largest HQ countries and six most represented capabilities within the selected geography.

The network chart counts independent hub organisations with a published location and investors with a published office/contact city. Associated hub sites, meeting-only locations and country research associations are excluded. A multi-country organisation can appear in several country rows. Directory coverage is incomplete.

The events chart covers the current month and following eleven months, counting each confirmed dated edition once by its start month. Ongoing editions are counted in the current month. Past editions and dates TBA are excluded. Future editions remain available in Events; zero means no confirmed edition in our current database.

Figures refresh with each catalogue build. These charts describe the available records; the dataset continues to expand.

How these numbers are counted

Reported round volume is the sum of source-backed, positive equity round amounts converted to EUR. Announced and signed rounds are not necessarily cash received; choose “Closed only” to narrow the view. Approximate figures are included and marked ≈; ceilings, lower bounds, targets and first closes are excluded.

Venture & growth includes explicitly labelled seed, angel, Series, venture, growth and equity funding/financing rounds. All primary equity also includes IPOs, public placements and other capital increases, including diversified groups. A company’s total is recorded funding in this view, not its lifetime funding or spending on drones.

Grants, debt, mixed instruments, convertibles, acquisitions, secondary transactions and noncash capital are excluded. Extensions and linked child records are excluded to avoid adding an extension to a potentially cumulative parent amount. All chart amounts are EUR equivalents; see the exchange-rate note below. Missing amounts are not zero; coverage is incomplete and these figures are not market size.

Countries use company headquarters. Domains form three mutually exclusive company groups, so a company operating in both domains is counted once. Investor rankings count named participations in the quantified rounds in view across all currencies, not investor cheque sizes. A round can have several investors; lender, grantor, arranger, adviser and underwriter roles are excluded.

Periods use the disclosed round year. Undated rounds appear in all-time totals but not the annual chart. Future-dated records are excluded. Records are deduplicated by round ID. All charts can be inspected as tables.
